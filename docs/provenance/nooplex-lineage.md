# Noöplex Lineage and Provenance

## Purpose

This record separates the identity and intended meaning of the Noöplex hyperconnectome from the incomplete historical artifacts currently available to this research branch.

## Current naming authority

**Source fact — Patrick's current correction, 2026-08-19:** the design is the **Noöplex hyperconnectome**. “Positronic brain” is inspiration, not the origin, name, identity, or technical classification of the design.

Consequences for this repository:

- The canonical research subject is `Noöplex` / `Noöplex hyperconnectome`.
- Asimov's positronic brain may appear in an inspiration or comparative-history note.
- The archived conversation title `Positronic brain origin` is only a retrieval locator. Its title is not evidence that the Noöplex originated there or should be described as a positronic-brain implementation.
- HC-1R is derived from the recovered Noöplex lineage plus new research. It is not an attempt to reproduce Asimov's fictional hardware.

## Evidence classes

### Direct current evidence

1. GitHub repository `thebrazenbeard/vera-brain`.
2. Initial commit `b84d45a0231ac6c96694273ad51ce4e06b2863ce`, dated 2026-08-19.
3. Initial README text: `Vera's conceptual Noöplex hyperconnetome brain`.
4. Patrick's current instructions and corrections in the task that created this branch.

These sources directly establish the repository target, research-only scope, present terminology, permission to write a research branch, and requirement to challenge assumptions.

### Indirect recovered evidence

A readback of ChatGPT conversation `6a848908-899c-83ea-9472-e6b59c1fd5ed` reports that:

- an attachment named `Noöplex HC-1 and HC-2 Architectures.pdf` existed in that chat;
- an archived conversation with the locator title `Positronic brain origin` contained HC-1/HC-2 text and later HC-3 material;
- HC-1 was described as analog/digital spiking neuromorphic layers with memristive or phase-change-memory synapses;
- HC-2 added quantum coprocessing;
- HC-3 added a synthetic neuroendocrine or modulatory system;
- Wreckforge material used Noöplex systems in server and shipmind roles, including a Noöplex-Q designation.

The same readback preserves a detailed critique of the HC-1/HC-2 attachment. It identified event-driven computation, near-memory processing, sparse communication, nonvolatile weights, heterogeneous regions, and embodied training as the strongest ideas. It identified missing memory architecture, interconnect, compiler/runtime, device calibration, fault modeling, and evidence classification as major weaknesses. It rejected generic quantum acceleration and co-locating a millikelvin QPU inside a humanoid head.

This is useful lineage evidence, but it is not a byte-identical recovery of the PDF or archived conversations.

## Recovered architecture generations

The following is an **indirect reconstruction**, not a canonical version history:

| Generation | Indirectly recovered description | Treatment in HC-1R |
|---|---|---|
| HC-1 | Heterogeneous analog/digital spiking neuromorphic system; memristive/PCM synaptic storage | Retain the heterogeneous, event-driven, and near-memory direction; add explicit cognition, memory, runtime, network, safety, reliability, and embodiment layers |
| HC-2 | HC-1 plus quantum coprocessing | Demote quantum resources to optional external services for narrow, evidenced workloads |
| HC-3 | HC-2 plus synthetic neuroendocrine/modulatory mechanisms | Preserve the regulatory insight as a typed, bounded neuromodulatory and salience control plane; do not require literal hormones |
| HC-1R | Research revision introduced in this repository | New proposed reference architecture; must not be misrepresented as recovered historical fact |

## Provenance rules for future work

1. Preserve recovered artifacts unchanged if they later become available.
2. Store interpretation and correction in separate documents or clearly marked sections.
3. Identify a statement as `SOURCE FACT`, `USER STATEMENT`, `DESIGN INFERENCE`, `PROPOSAL`, or `OPEN QUESTION` when ambiguity matters.
4. Use current corrections over misleading archival titles.
5. Never use archive chronology alone to infer authority, canonicity, or origin.
6. Do not convert inspiration into lineage.
7. Do not convert a fictional description into a scientific claim.
8. Do not convert a demonstrated component into a demonstrated system.

## Recovery status

`DEGRADED_BOUNDED`

The recovered material is sufficient to identify the broad HC-1/HC-2/HC-3 design direction and to construct a separately labeled HC-1R proposal. It is insufficient for verbatim historical reproduction or claims about the exact wording, diagrams, references, or intended priority of every original subsystem.
